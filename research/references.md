# References

The keyed, annotated bibliography for the garment-measurement feasibility study.

**How to read the entries**
- **Type:** standard · guide · peer-reviewed · book · patent · vendor (datasheet, product page) · practitioner (blog, inspection company, trade press).
- **Access:** what was actually examined for this study.
  - *Full text* — the document was read.
  - *Preview* — official or authorised excerpt, e.g. an iTeh standards preview containing the scope, definitions and some clauses.
  - *Abstract* — only the abstract was read.
  - *Secondary* — the claim was taken from another source that reports it; treat as weaker.
- **Supports:** the claims in this study that rest on the source.

Paywalled standards were not purchased. Where only a preview was available, only claims visible in the preview are attributed to the standard.
