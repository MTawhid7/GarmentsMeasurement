# References

The consolidated bibliography, v2. It merges the sources of both earlier research bodies, corrects titles, authors and attributions, and states **what was actually examined** for each source.

**Access status — what was actually read:**
- **Full text** — the document itself was read.
- **Preview** — an official or authorised excerpt (e.g. an iTeh standards preview with scope, definitions and some clauses).
- **Abstract** — only the abstract or summary was read.
- **Secondary** — the claim comes from another source reporting it, including search-engine summaries of pages that could not be retrieved directly. Treat as weaker.

Claims are attributed to a source only as far as its access status allows. Paywalled standards were not purchased.
